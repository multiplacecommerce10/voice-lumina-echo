# Conscious Voice Journey

Create a high-converting, elegant, premium one-page landing page for an international online course called “The Power of Conscious Voice”, created by Cuca Medina and presented under the Tecendo Som brand.

The landing page must feel artistic, sophisticated, human, luminous and conversion-oriented. It should not look like a generic online course page. It should feel like a premium artistic education experience.

BRAND / VISUAL DIRECTION:

- Use a dark, cinematic background with black and deep purple tones.

- Use glowing accents in warm golden-orange and electric cyan/blue.

- The page should visually match the campaign image style: mystical, musical, vibrant, elegant, with luminous voice-energy aesthetics.

- Include a small Tecendo Som logo in the header.

- Use modern, elegant typography with strong contrast and excellent readability.

- The design should be responsive and mobile-friendly.

- Include subtle glowing effects, refined dividers and premium spacing.

- Avoid clutter and avoid a cheap marketing look.

PRIMARY GOAL:

Convert visitors into paying students for:

1. Live Founding Cohort — US$147/month

2. Replay-Only Pass — US$97/month

SECONDARY GOAL:

Encourage eligible students to visit a separate scholarship page through a button linking to:

“Global Solidarity Scholarships”

TARGET AUDIENCE:

- singers

- music students

- voice students

- music educators

- speakers

- artists

- sensitive creators

- adults seeking body awareness, resonance and expressive freedom

- international students, especially from Europe, UK, East Coast US, India, Pakistan and other regions
